# Public launch blockers

The product story and evidence surfaces are ready for public review. These
owner-supplied facts must be completed before treating the site as a legally
finished public service; they cannot be inferred safely from code.

- Name the operating person or legal entity in Terms, Privacy, and the footer.
- Add the entity's registered address, country/jurisdiction, and governing law.
- Confirm that `legal@betglitch.com`, `privacy@betglitch.com`, and
  `support@betglitch.com` are monitored.
- Name the football/probability provider publicly only after confirming the
  provider contract permits attribution and downstream display.
- List the actual hosting, email, analytics, and error-monitoring processors in
  Privacy, with concrete retention periods and lawful bases.
- Add an account-deletion route and a password-reset route, or remove account
  registration until both support paths exist.
- Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review for gambling-related content,
  marketing, age gating, and responsible-gambling obligations.

Do not replace these items with generic wording. Public transparency includes
being explicit about who operates the service and how user data is handled.
