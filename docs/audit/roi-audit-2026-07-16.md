## Findings

**Machine-generated (rule engine):**

1. **UI-matching 95% CI includes zero or negative** (−14.02% → +10.25%). Point estimate −1.98% ROI. Cannot publish a specific ROI figure.

**Hand-added by audit author (updated post-backfill):**

2. **The published 19% ROI was an artifact of the back-calc-from-EV fallback.** Pre-2026-05-13 rows (205 of 275, 74.5% of universe) had `PredictionLog.odds = NULL`. `PredictionLog.calculate_performance()` (`core/models.py:258-261`) fell back to `market_odds = (EV + 1) / confidence` — the model's own optimistic EV back-solved to an implied odds. This produced win payouts systematically higher than any real bookmaker would pay. Backfill with real median odds (SportMonks, `market_id=80, total='2.5'`, median across 6-10 bookmakers) moved ROI from +19% to −2% for UI-matching, +3% for unfiltered.

3. **The model wins 58.6% of the time but at odds too short to be profitable.** Median winning odds are ~1.50–1.70. Break-even at 58.6% win rate requires average odds of 1.71. The model is essentially at bookmaker break-even, tilting slightly negative once vig is priced in. The ROI is *not* a mismeasurement problem — it's a real edge problem.

4. **Single-market concentration remains (Q5).** 97% of UI-matching bets are Over/Under 2.5. Post-backfill, that market shows −2.21% ROI (was +16.52% at back-calc odds). The `1x2` market loses money at −3.75% UI / −21.29% unfiltered. BetGlitch is empirically an Over/Under 2.5 specialist, and the specialty is not currently profitable.

5. **Filtered-out 0.55–0.60 bucket is the only profitable bucket (Q4).** 57 unfiltered bets in that range yield **+29.89% ROI** — the strongest signal in the entire audit. The current UI threshold `confidence ≥ 0.60` filters this out. The 0.60–0.70 bucket (206 bets) is where all UI-matching rows sit and shows −2.71% ROI. The current filter is actively cutting the platform's best data.

6. **Wide CI at n=210 (Q2).** UI CI is 24 pp wide (−14% to +10%). Even a "would we publish this?" test needs the CI entirely on one side of zero; we're not close.

7. **Monthly volatility increased with real odds (Q3).** Only 3/6 months positive (was 5/6). Two consecutive losing months (Mar −27%, Apr −14%) with real odds. The winning months (May +37%, Jun +83% on n=1) may not persist.

8. **Empty high-confidence buckets remain (Q4, Q8).** 0.80–1.00 bucket contains 0 bets in both variants. Model calibration issue unchanged.

9. **Per-league claims are still noise-level (Q6).** All 10 top-volume leagues underpowered. Post-backfill, the losing leagues (Serie B −44%, Eliteserien −28%, Premier League −25%, La Liga −17%) balance the winners; net effect on total ROI is close to zero.
